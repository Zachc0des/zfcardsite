Extra mileage · make every card work harder

Promotion recommendations.

A card is a start — here's how to turn it into a system.

Add a QR code

Point it at your booking page, portfolio or Linktree. One scan beats typing any URL.

Match your socials

Use the same colors and fonts on your Instagram highlights, TikTok banner and LinkedIn header so people recognize you everywhere.

Card + resume combo

Job hunting? Staple nothing — hand your card with your resume so your contact info survives the paper pile.

Leave-behind stacks

Local shops, gyms, community boards and job sites often let small businesses leave cards. Ten stacks of ten beats one stack of a hundred.

Write on the back

Jot a quote, discount code or "mentioned you'd call Tuesday" before handing it over — personalized cards get kept.

Digital twin

Get a matching digital card image for email signatures, DMs and story posts — same design, zero printing cost.
